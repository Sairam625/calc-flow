Key Takeaway

  • Using eggshell waste significantly reduces the carbon footprint, energy and water demand compared to conventional limestone-derived CaCO₃.
  • It diverts food waste from landfills, reduces mining and land disturbance, and supports a circular economy.
  • The main environmental trade-offs are the energy and water used during washing, drying and grinding, which are much lower than those in limestone processing.
Less mining. Less waste. Lower emissions. A more circular future.

Cross-Industry Applications of Upcycled CaCO₃

CONSTRUCTION

Eco-Concrete & Precast

Acts as a reactive micro-filler in green concrete, accelerating early compressive strength and reducing Portland cement clinker content.

POLYMERS & MASTERBATCHES

Polymers & Masterbatches

Sub-75 µm calcite powder enhances stiffness, heat distortion temperature, and surface finish in polypropylene and PVC extrusion.

PAPER & COATINGS

Paper Coating & Pulp

High whiteness (L* > 94) and alkaline sizing capability provide opacity, brightness, and ink receptivity for recycled paper products.

AGRONOMY

Soil Conditioning & Agriculture

Grade B material rapidly neutralizes acidic agricultural soils, enriching root zones with bio-available calcium without soil compaction.

ANIMAL NUTRITION

Animal Nutrition Supplement

Sterilized, pathogen-free eggshell calcium offers superior biological assimilation for poultry feed pre-mixes.

PAINTS & RESINS

Paints, Sealants & Adhesives

Functional extender offering precise rheology control, scrub resistance, and matte sheen uniformity in industrial coatings.
